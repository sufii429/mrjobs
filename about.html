About MrJobs

What this board is, and how to use it.

What is MrJobs?

MrJobs is a free job board for Medical Representative, Territory Manager and Area Manager positions in Pakistan’s pharmaceutical industry. Listings come from hiring announcements shared by pharmaceutical companies, and every listing links you straight to the employer on WhatsApp.

How to apply

  1. Browse the listings on the home page, or search by title, company or city.
  2. Tap Apply Now on any listing.
  3. WhatsApp opens with the employer’s number — send your CV directly.
Good to know: listings are published as shared by the hiring companies. Always confirm the role, location and package with the employer before applying — and never pay anyone for a job offer.

Stay in the loop

New openings are added regularly. Subscribe to MrJobs Weekly and the latest medical-rep jobs land in your inbox every week.
