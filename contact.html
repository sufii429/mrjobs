Contact MrJobs

The fastest way to reach us depends on who you are.

Employers — post a vacancy (free)

Use the “Are You Hiring?” form on the home page — Company, Job Title, City, Apply-contact and Details are required, and you confirm you are an employer before submitting. Have a poster instead? Email it with your company name to sufyanbarret@gmail.com and we take care of the rest.

Job seekers — apply on the board

Browse the listings, tap Apply Now, and WhatsApp opens with the employer’s number — send your CV directly. Please do not email your CV to the vacancy address above — it is for employers posting jobs only.

Newsletter

MrJobs Weekly goes out every Monday with the newest openings. Subscribe here — unsubscribe anytime by replying to any issue.

Anything else

Corrections to a listing, a privacy question, or feedback on the board: sufyanbarret@gmail.com. We usually reply within a day or two.

Good to know: listings are published as shared by hiring companies — always confirm the role, location and package with the employer before applying, and never pay anyone for a job offer.
