Privacy Notice

What we collect, who processes it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Newsletter signup (name, email, optional city) — used only to send the weekly MrJobs jobs email. Nothing else.
  • Vacancy submissions (company name, job title, city, contact details, job description) — the contact details are published in the listing so candidates can apply, exactly as the employer provided them.

Who processes it

  • Forms are handled by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which retains submissions for up to 30 days.
  • The site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-QPQCFWE9EP) to count visits — Google processes that data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and Cookies

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. Users can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Job-seeker details are never collected by MrJobs — applications go directly to the employer via the listing’s Apply button (WhatsApp or email). We never see your CV.

Unsubscribe / removal

To stop the weekly email, reply “unsubscribe” to any newsletter email. To have your data or a listing removed, email sufyanbarret@gmail.com and we will take care of it.
