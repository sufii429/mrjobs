Hilton Pharma Jobs — Medical Representative & Territory Manager Openings

MrJobs Blog · Published July 12, 2025 · Updated October 2026

Hilton Pharma is one of Pakistan’s established pharmaceutical companies, known for strong specialty portfolios and a nationwide field force. Its Medical Representative and Territory Manager vacancies are posted on MrJobs as hiring managers share them — this page shows the current opening, what Hilton listings usually require, and how to apply.

📍 Current Hilton Pharma Opening on MrJobs

This Hilton Pharma listing is open on the board right now. Listings stay live for 28 days, and new ones are added as they arrive — if it has expired by the time you read this, check the All Jobs page or the Faisalabad jobs page for the latest vacancies.

  • Territory Manager (TM) — Diabetes Portfolio — Faisalabad

Hilton recruits field force across Pakistan for portfolios such as Diabetes, general and cardiology lines. Openings in other cities — Lahore, Multan, Rawalpindi and beyond — appear on the board whenever the company is hiring there.

📌 Required Qualifications

Hilton Pharma listings on MrJobs typically ask for:

  • Graduation (Science preferred)
  • 1–2 years of pharma sales experience for Territory Manager roles (fresh candidates may apply where a listing says so)
  • Confidence in communication and product detailing
  • Own bike and licence preferred

📝 How to Apply?

Open the Hilton Pharma listing above and tap Apply Now — the WhatsApp button on each listing opens a chat with the hiring manager, with your job reference already written in. For every current opening across all companies, see the All Jobs page.

📍 Insider Tip: Hilton prefers field force with a past track record. Keep your last performance data handy for the interview.
