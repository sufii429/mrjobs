Hilton Pharma Hiring Medical Representatives — July 2025

MrJobs Blog · Published July 12, 2025

Hilton Pharma, a leading name in Pakistan’s pharmaceutical industry, has announced fresh job openings for Medical Representatives in July 2025. The company is actively expanding its reach through new product launches and therapy areas.

📍 Available Cities for Hiring

  • Lahore: MR positions in general and cardiology lines
  • Multan: New teams for rural areas being formed
  • Rawalpindi: Multiple districts being covered for promotion
  • Hyderabad: Hiring for experienced MR with GP panel

📌 Required Qualifications

  • 1-2 years of sales experience in pharma (fresh may also apply)
  • Confidence in communication and product detailing
