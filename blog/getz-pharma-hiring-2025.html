Getz Pharma Hiring Medical Representatives in 2025

MrJobs Blog · Published July 12, 2025

In 2025, Getz Pharma has launched a large-scale hiring drive for Medical Representatives across Pakistan. The company has announced new product lines and is strengthening its sales teams in both major cities and rural areas.

🔍 Where Are Jobs Available?

  • Karachi: Head office hiring + new field force for South region
  • Lahore: MR positions in general and specialty lines
  • Islamabad/Rawalpindi: Multiple territory managers also being shortlisted
  • Multan, Faisalabad, Peshawar: Active hiring in Q3 and Q4

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • Graduation in any discipline (BSc/Pharm-D preferred)
  • Fresh or experienced candidates welcome
  • Strong communication and sales skills
  • Willingness to travel within assigned area

📱 How to Apply?

You can find updated MR openings on our MrJobs main page or apply directly via WhatsApp link provided with each listing.

💡 Tip: Always carry updated CV, recent photo, and note down your last sales territory and manager info during the interview.
