Getz Pharma Jobs — Medical Representative & Territory Manager Openings

MrJobs Blog · Published July 12, 2025 · Updated October 2026

Getz Pharma is one of Pakistan’s largest pharmaceutical companies and a regular hirer of field-force talent. Its Medical Representative and Territory Manager vacancies appear on MrJobs as soon as area managers share them — this page explains where Getz is hiring, what its listings usually ask for, and how to apply.

🔍 Current Getz Pharma Openings on MrJobs

These Getz Pharma listings are open on the board right now. Listings stay live for 28 days, and new ones are added as they arrive — if a link below has expired, check the All Jobs page for the latest Getz vacancies.

  • Territory Manager, Insulin (Insuget) Portfolio — Faisalabad
  • Territory Manager (TM) — Gastro Physician Group — Faisalabad
  • Territory Manager (TM) — Ortho Group — Faisalabad
  • Territory Manager — Jhang

Most recent Getz hiring on MrJobs has been for Territory Manager roles in specialty portfolios — Insulin (Insuget), Gastro and Ortho — based in Faisalabad and Jhang. Karachi head-office and other regional roles appear on the board from time to time.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

Getz Pharma listings on MrJobs typically ask for:

  • Graduation in any discipline (BSc/Pharm-D preferred)
  • Fresh or experienced candidates, depending on the listing
  • Strong communication and sales skills
  • Willingness to travel within the assigned territory

📱 How to Apply?

Open any Getz Pharma listing above and tap Apply Now — each listing carries a WhatsApp button that opens a chat with the hiring manager, with your job reference already written in. You can also browse every current opening on the All Jobs page.

💡 Tip: Always carry an updated CV and a recent photo to the interview, and note down your last sales territory and manager’s name beforehand.
