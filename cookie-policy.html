Which cookies this site uses, what they do, and your choices.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website or its service providers save in your browser. They let a service remember a visitor between pages and visits. Similar storage in your browser (called local storage) works in much the same way.

Cookies set by MrJobs itself

  • MrJobs sets no cookies of its own, and its own scripts do not use browser storage to track you.
  • The single local-storage entry this site keeps is your cookie-banner choice (mrjobs-cookie-consent), saved only so the banner does not reappear on every page after you press Accept. It contains nothing but the word “accepted” and is never sent anywhere.

Analytics cookies — Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-QPQCFWE9EP) to count visits and see which pages are useful. When it loads, Google may set these cookies in your browser:

  • _ga — distinguishes one visitor from another; lasts 2 years.
  • _ga_QPQCFWE9EP — keeps the state of a visit session; lasts 2 years.
  • _gid — distinguishes visitors for short-term counting; lasts 24 hours.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits with the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Advertising cookies — Google AdSense

  • This site loads Google’s advertising script (AdSense). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. Users can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Forms

The newsletter signup and vacancy submission forms are handled by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) on its own service. What data those forms collect, and how to unsubscribe or ask for removal, is covered in our Privacy Notice.

Your choices

  • Browser settings: every modern browser lets you block or delete cookies and site storage. The site keeps working if you block them — only visit counting and ad personalisation stop.
  • Reset the banner: clearing this site’s browser data removes your saved Accept choice, and the cookie banner will be shown again on your next visit.
  • Opt-outs: personalised ads can be switched off at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info, and analytics measurement with the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below. Questions about cookies or privacy: email mrjobs.top@gmail.com.
